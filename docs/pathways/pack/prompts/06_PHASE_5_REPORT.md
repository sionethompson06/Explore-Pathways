# PHASE 5 PROMPT - The Discovery Report, Template-Complete First
Implement the family-facing report from Specification 05 using approved templates and deterministic report input. Do not enable live AI yet. This phase must create a valuable, complete product without any model key.

Implement R01 starting point, R02 combined insight, R03 up to two credible directions, R04 up to two opportunities, R05 important planning questions, R06 preliminary pathway sketch and R07 available next action. Normal target 500-650 visible words; do not pad a low-information report or hide essential qualifications to meet the target.

Personalization uses stated goals and at least two supported facts for a combined insight. Otherwise use a straightforward summary. Attribute academic claims to the parent's report. Do not infer emotion, diagnosis, eligibility or family purchasing ability. Use recognition and possibility, never guilt, fear, false urgency or promised future outcomes.

Public labels: Worth exploring; Worth exploring with considerations; More information would help. No scores, Strong Potential Fit, named providers, invented course lists, fake school confirmation, guaranteed time savings or future-school fallback. Material caveats are readable beside the relevant card.

Provide a useful preview, visible Continue without saving, optional Save my report and a truthful consultation action determined by service mode. Do not blur essential content or force a phone/email wall. Service scope and any paid Blueprint distinction must be clear. Save CTA may link to an honest not-yet-configured state until Phase 6; do not pretend data was emailed.

Store immutable-by-edit report snapshots linked to profile/rules/content/template versions, subject to retention/deletion. Reopening a snapshot does not regenerate content. Edited answers produce a new revision. Public payload excludes internal evidence and lead scoring.

Acceptance: complete realistic reports for K-4, middle, HS athlete, credit review, mixed academics and unknown cases; empty/conflict/fallback states; no unsupported sentence; mobile/desktop visual QA; readable qualifications; no fabricated report insight; every opportunity tied to actual interest; CTA reflects actual configured availability.
