# Specification 01 - Product Constitution, normalized
## Product
Pathways is an Education Pathways Ecosystem: family discovery, educational navigation, a professional Student Success Blueprint, implementation assistance, and ongoing support. An eventual independent mastery school fills documented gaps in partner offerings; it is not the default destination.

The launch is Model 2. Model 3 remains a three-to-five-year destination subject to approval and actual school readiness. Business entity, DBA and board structure remain undecided. Do not publish invented affiliation, tax status, accreditation, school authority or school availability.

## Audience
The app welcomes K-12 families while marketing emphasizes grades 5-12. Internal bands: ELEMENTARY K-4, MIDDLE 5-8, HIGH_SCHOOL 9-12, UNDETERMINED. These are branching categories, not assertions about local school organization. Nonstandard grade or location receives a useful general exploration and/or human contact route, not an invented enrollment determination.

## Promise
Help parents understand possible directions and the next questions to investigate. Recognition -> possibility -> relief -> confidence -> agency. Emotional relevance must use stated goals, not inferred vulnerability. Never use fear about a child's future, guilt, invented lost opportunities, manufactured urgency, or guaranteed results.

## Products and authority
Discovery is free preliminary information, not an academic assessment, legal opinion or verified placement. An initial consultation clarifies priorities and service fit. A paid Blueprint adds professional investigation and planning. Continuing services require their own agreed scope. The MVP only offers services marked operationally available.

The school of record, where one exists, retains its applicable instructional, credit and transcript authority. The parent portal is a planning record, not an official transcript or credential. Partner-first means evaluate available alternatives before an owned school; no suitable algorithm result is not proof of an unavailable marketplace.

## MVP users
Guest parent; verified parent; assigned advisor; authorized administrator. Children are subjects of parent profiles, not student-account holders in this release. No unauthenticated staff panel or self-assigned staff role. One student is profiled per Discovery session; the model permits multiple students per guardian later without exposing cross-family records.

## Success
Families understand a useful next direction and the limits of the report, then choose whether to save or consult. Measure attended useful consultations and advisor correction rates, not clicks alone. No purchasing propensity in educational fit. No hidden referral commissions influencing suggestions.

## Not yet
No courses, diagnostics integrations, student AI tutoring, grades, legal residency decisions, school enrollment, NCAA decisions, reclassification recommendations, payments, record uploads, Blueprint authoring, public provider directory, provider portal, or autonomous outreach.
