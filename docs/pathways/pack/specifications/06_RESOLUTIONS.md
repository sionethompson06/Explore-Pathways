# Engineering Reconciliation and Decision Register
This is a proposed canonical build contract. Phase 0 must surface these changes for owner acceptance rather than silently applying a rewrite.

## Resolutions carried from the latest decisions
1. K-12 supported; grades 5-12 marketing emphasis. Grade-band logic includes K-4 and UNDETERMINED.
2. Specification 05 supersedes older mandatory-email walls. A useful guest report is available without saving; save/return uses verified contact.
3. Public labels do not use Strong Potential Fit or percentages in the pilot. Internal numbers are provisional, not psychometric confidence.
4. No live Pathways school, automatic school fallback, named provider recommendation or assured local eligibility in Discovery.
5. AI has two narrow writing slots, not authority to choose models or qualifications. No AI requirement for a functional report.
6. Actual provider, business and legal details must be configured/approved. An attractive mockup is not evidence of staffing, partnerships or outcomes.

## Additional builder-review corrections proposed here
7. Add explicit school-change preference, unavailable times, delivery preference, subject advancement and real adult availability. Prior drafts used some facts without collecting them.
8. Allow nickname/skip display name and optional ZIP. Do not collect unnecessary precise identifiers to generate marketing text.
9. Use stable canonical IDs and import aliases. Do not mix base-model P-series IDs with overlays or inconsistent S09 labels.
10. Replace uncapped additive scoring with within-group deduplication; remove score-only public thresholds. At least two independent scored dimensions support a card. Show no card rather than fill empty space.
11. No strong travel-frequency inference from 'travel interest'. No 'math advanced' inference from 'generally ahead'. No 'afternoons unavailable' inference from 'morning preferred'. No 'limited supervision' inference from low desired parental involvement.
12. Graduation concern does not automatically mean missing credits. College desire does not mean AP/college-course readiness. School-change credit review includes grade 9.
13. Do not rank all private online implementations as inherently superior or assume hybrid means both live online and on-campus. Missing capabilities remain review items.
14. Rule engine, content and reported learning observations remain distinct; learning preferences are not diagnostic 'learning styles'.
15. Report storage/versioning is subordinate to approved deletion/retention, not indefinite student-data retention.
16. Advisory/sales workflow is independent of school model and recommendation rank.

## Items owner must decide before live launch, not blockers for a synthetic demo
- Repository/branch/deployment project and authorized production promoter.
- Legal operating entity, final brand, contact address, privacy notice and service terms.
- Which services, grades and markets can actually be staffed; whether consultation is free, its duration, and scheduling windows.
- Authentication/email/scheduler provider selection and approved data-processing terms.
- Guest and saved-record retention periods; deletion procedures, log/back-up policies and owner.
- AI provider, data handling, permissions, cost ceiling and whether AI is necessary for launch.
- Media use rights, verified advisor credentials and any authorized affiliations.

No default legal approval is implied. Use synthetic fixtures and explicit service-unavailable states until required decisions are supplied. Continue independent work within scope; do not fabricate missing settings.
