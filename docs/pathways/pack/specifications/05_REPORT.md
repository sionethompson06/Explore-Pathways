# Specification 05 - Discovery Report and Conversion, normalized
## Commercial purpose
Earn the next conversation by giving real preliminary clarity. The report is a complete free exploration, not a paywall teaser. Paid value is research, verification, coordination and professional planning; do not intentionally hide basic safety limitations or fabricate an unresolved problem to create a sale.

## Seven sections
R01: Student's starting point. Optional nickname, a goal-relevant headline, short attributed summary, up to three actual priorities. Visible scope: Based on your answers; not a school placement or academic assessment.
R02: One useful insight connecting two supplied facts. If unsupported, a plain summary; no invented 'aha'.
R03: Usually two credible model directions. Each has title, allowed fit label, why it connects, and what needs review. One or zero is valid. No named providers.
R04: Up to two opportunity signals supported by explicit interests; each has EXPLORE_NOW, NEXT_REVIEW, or LONGER_TERM_EXPLORATION and NOT_ELIGIBLE scope. No promised program opening or earned readiness.
R05: One to three important planning questions. Do not bury a material concern solely to meet the count.
R06: Clearly preliminary pathway sketch: generic model + support + interests. No made-up course schedule, grades, graduation percentage, credits or assigned advisor.
R07: A personal invitation to an available next step; save is secondary. Appointment duration, price and availability come from approved configuration. Explain that a detailed Blueprint is a separate paid service, with actual pricing only when approved.

Working length: 500-650 visible words, excluding useful optional detail. This is a testable content target, not a promise of optimized conversion. Critical qualifications take precedence over a word cap.

## Emotional language
Recognize declared goals and preserve choice. Do not infer parental fear, a student's future potential, income, diagnosis or desperation. No fear, guilt, pressure, manufactured countdown, guaranteed scholarships, promised time saved, or claims that traditional schools harm the child. Credit the parent report: You described / You told us / You want to explore.

## AI boundary
The complete report must work with approved templates and no model key. Optional AI may produce only R01's summary and R02's insight. Titles, model choices, opportunities, reviews, qualifiers and CTA selection remain deterministic.

AI receives only approved minimal facts, content IDs and evidence IDs, using 'your student' rather than a name. Exclude parent contact, exact location, free text, sales intent and internal-only notes. Require structured output with evidence IDs; validate meaning as well as syntax. Evidence IDs alone do not prove truthful wording. When doubtful, discard and use template content.

No live browsing, tool actions, provider lookup, enrollment, autonomous messaging, grading or eligibility decision from the language model. No repeated generation on each page view. Save the generated text with its model/prompt/version metadata; derive hash and idempotency key from the same profile/rules/content/template versions. Configured timeout or invalid content -> approved fallback, not blank results.

## Contact and saving
Guest can read a useful preview and continue to the complete report without email. Offer Save and return anytime; account persistence follows verified email, not mere entry of another person's address. Keep saved report private. No report details in subject lines or pre-authentication emails. Email delivery request, marketing email opt-in and any SMS permission are separate. No marketing checkbox preselected. No child data in ads, URLs or replay tools.

## Conversion states
Default CTA when consultation is live: Explore [student]'s options with an advisor. After verified booking: Prepare for the conversation. When only a contact request can be supported: Request a conversation; never show 'booked'. When neither is operating: clear interest/availability state, only collect interest if that collection is approved.

No calendar-account connection required from parents. Save a request if supported even when the scheduler is down, without representing it as an appointment. Never promise no-cost, duration, response speed, advisor identity or slots absent approved settings.

## Empty and error states
Insufficient answers -> useful priorities and clarifying questions.
Conflicting needs -> neutral visible tradeoff.
Unverified local programs -> model exploration, not program availability.
AI failure -> full deterministic report.
Expired guest session -> explanation and safe restart; no exposure via guessed ID.
Scheduling failure -> report preserved and truthful retry/contact option.
Unavailable future school -> never advertise it as the solution.

## Sample copy, only for fictional fixture cases
Headline: More room for basketball. A clear academic direction.
Insight: Your answers suggest that flexibility matters, but regular academic check-ins still do too. The next question is how a program combines scheduling freedom with accessible support, rather than whether it is simply called online.
CTA: Let's explore what could work for your student.
Scope: These are education models to investigate. Academic records, individual programs and enrollment eligibility have not been reviewed.
